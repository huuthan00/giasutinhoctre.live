# Bai tap thuc hanh C++

## Cap do 1: Lam quen

1. In ra man hinh ten, tuoi, lop, mon hoc yeu thich.
2. Nhap chieu dai va chieu rong, tinh chu vi va dien tich hinh chu nhat.
3. Nhap diem Toan, Van, Anh, tinh diem trung binh.
4. Nhap so phut, doi thanh gio va phut.
5. Nhap gia tien va so luong, tinh tong tien.

## Cap do 2: Dieu kien

1. Kiem tra mot so la chan hay le.
2. Nhap diem va in xep loai: gioi, kha, trung binh, yeu.
3. Kiem tra mot nam co phai nam nhuan khong.
4. Nhap 3 so, in ra so lon nhat.
5. Viet may tinh mini: nhap 2 so va phep tinh `+ - * /`.

## Cap do 3: Vong lap

1. In cac so tu 1 den n.
2. Tinh tong cac so tu 1 den n.
3. In bang cuu chuong cua mot so.
4. Kiem tra mot so co phai so nguyen to khong.
5. Viet game doan so: may co mot so bi mat, nguoi choi doan den khi dung.

## Cap do 4: Ham

1. Viet ham `int square(int x)` tra ve binh phuong.
2. Viet ham `bool isEven(int x)` kiem tra chan le.
3. Viet ham `double average(double a, double b, double c)`.
4. Viet ham `bool isPrime(int n)`.
5. Viet chuong trinh co menu, moi lua chon goi mot ham rieng.

## Cap do 5: Mang va vector

1. Nhap n diem, tinh diem trung binh.
2. Tim diem cao nhat va thap nhat.
3. Dem co bao nhieu diem lon hon hoac bang 8.
4. Nhap danh sach ten bang `vector<string>` va tim ten theo tu khoa.
5. Lam todo list: them viec, xem danh sach, danh dau hoan thanh.

## Cap do 6: Struct va file

1. Tao `struct Student` gom `name`, `age`, `score`.
2. Nhap danh sach hoc sinh va in bang thong tin.
3. Tim hoc sinh co diem cao nhat.
4. Sap xep hoc sinh theo diem giam dan.
5. Ghi danh sach hoc sinh ra file text va doc lai.
