# Khoa hoc lap trinh C++ cho hoc sinh 15 tuoi

## Muc tieu

Khoa hoc nay duoc thiet ke cho hoc sinh 15 tuoi moi bat dau lap trinh. Sau khoa hoc, hoc sinh co the:

- Viet va chay chuong trinh C++ co cau truc ro rang.
- Hieu bien, kieu du lieu, dieu kien, vong lap, ham, mang, chuoi, vector va struct.
- Biet tach bai toan thanh cac buoc nho, kiem thu tung phan va sua loi co he thong.
- Lam duoc cac bai tap thuat toan co ban va mot du an nho co tinh ung dung.
- Doc duoc loi bien dich, loi logic don gian va tu cai tien code cua minh.

## Doi tuong

- Tuoi: 15.
- Kien thuc dau vao: biet dung may tinh, go phim co ban, chua can biet lap trinh.
- Thoi luong goi y: 12 tuan, moi tuan 2 buoi, moi buoi 90 phut.
- Si so phu hop: 6-12 hoc sinh de giao vien co the theo sat tung ban.

## Phuong phap day

- Moi buoi gom 4 phan: khoi dong ngan, giai thich bang vi du, thuc hanh co huong dan, bai tap tu lam.
- Uu tien bai toan gan voi doi song cua hoc sinh: tinh diem, tro choi doan so, quan ly danh sach, mini game console.
- Khong day cu phap qua dai. Moi khai niem phai di kem mot chuong trinh chay duoc.
- Moi 2 tuan co mot san pham nho de hoc sinh thay tien bo that.

- Game doan so nang cap.
- Quan ly diem hoc sinh.
- Tu dien mini Anh - Viet.

- Ung dung quan ly viec can lam.
