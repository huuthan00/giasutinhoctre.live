## Tuan 1: Khoi dong voi C++

### Buoi 1: Chuong trinh dau tien

- Muc tieu: biet chuong trinh C++ gom nhung phan nao va chay duoc file dau tien.
- Noi dung: `#include <iostream>`, `main()`, `cout`, `return 0`.
- Thuc hanh: in ten, tuoi, so thich tren nhieu dong.
- Bai tap ve nha: viet chuong trinh in thoi khoa bieu mot ngay.

### Buoi 2: Nhap du lieu

- Muc tieu: dung `cin` de nhap du lieu tu ban phim.
- Noi dung: bien `int`, `double`, `string`; cach dat ten bien.
- Thuc hanh: nhap ten va nam sinh, tinh tuoi gan dung.
- Bai tap ve nha: chuong trinh tinh chu vi va dien tich hinh chu nhat.

## Tuan 2: Dieu kien va phep toan

### Buoi 3: Tinh toan

- Muc tieu: dung toan tu `+ - * / %` va phan biet chia nguyen, chia thuc.
- Thuc hanh: tinh diem trung binh 3 mon.
- Bai tap: doi phut sang gio va phut; tinh tien mua hang.

### Buoi 4: Re nhanh if/else

- Muc tieu: viet dieu kien don gian va dieu kien ket hop.
- Noi dung: `if`, `else if`, `else`, `&&`, `||`, `!`.
- Thuc hanh: xep loai hoc luc theo diem.
- Bai tap: kiem tra nam nhuan, kiem tra so chan le.

## Tuan 3: Vong lap

### Buoi 5: Vong lap for

- Muc tieu: lap lai mot viec voi so lan biet truoc.
- Thuc hanh: in bang cuu chuong, tinh tong tu 1 den n.
- Bai tap: tinh giai thua, dem uoc cua mot so.

### Buoi 6: Vong lap while

- Muc tieu: lap khi chua biet truoc so lan.
- Thuc hanh: game doan so 1-100.
- Bai tap: nhap den khi gap so 0, tinh tong cac so da nhap.

## Tuan 4: Ham

### Buoi 7: Viet ham don gian

- Muc tieu: tach code thanh cac ham nho.
- Noi dung: tham so, gia tri tra ve, `void`.
- Thuc hanh: ham tinh dien tich, ham kiem tra chan le.

### Buoi 8: Giai bai bang ham

- Muc tieu: biet chon phan nao nen tach thanh ham.
- Thuc hanh: may tinh mini voi menu.
- Bai tap: viet ham `isPrime`, `maxOfThree`, `gradeLabel`.

## Tuan 5: Mang 1 chieu

### Buoi 9: Luu nhieu gia tri

- Muc tieu: khai bao, nhap, xuat mang.
- Thuc hanh: nhap diem cua n hoc sinh va tinh trung binh.

### Buoi 10: Xu ly mang

- Muc tieu: tim max, min, dem, tinh tong co dieu kien.
- Bai tap: dem so diem tren 8, tim diem cao nhat, tim vi tri diem thap nhat.

## Tuan 6: Chuoi

### Buoi 11: Lam viec voi string

- Muc tieu: nhap va xu ly chuoi co khoang trang.
- Noi dung: `getline`, `length`, duyet tung ky tu.
- Thuc hanh: dem so ky tu, chuan hoa loi chao.

### Buoi 12: Bai tap chuoi

- Muc tieu: ap dung dieu kien va vong lap voi chuoi.
- Thuc hanh: kiem tra mat khau manh yeu.
- Bai tap: dem nguyen am, dao nguoc chuoi, kiem tra palindrome don gian.

## Tuan 7: Vector

### Buoi 13: Danh sach co kich thuoc linh hoat

- Muc tieu: dung `vector` thay cho mang khi so phan tu thay doi.
- Noi dung: `push_back`, `size`, duyet bang chi so.
- Thuc hanh: danh sach viec can lam.

### Buoi 14: Menu quan ly danh sach

- Muc tieu: tao menu them, xem, xoa, tim.
- San pham: todo list chay tren console.

## Tuan 8: Struct

### Buoi 15: Gom du lieu thanh doi tuong

- Muc tieu: biet tao `struct` cho mot hoc sinh, mon hang, cong viec.
- Thuc hanh: struct `Student` gom ten, tuoi, diem.

### Buoi 16: Vector struct

- Muc tieu: quan ly nhieu doi tuong.
- San pham: ung dung quan ly hoc sinh don gian.

## Tuan 9: Tim kiem va sap xep

### Buoi 17: Tim kiem tuyen tinh

- Muc tieu: tim phan tu trong danh sach.
- Thuc hanh: tim hoc sinh theo ten, tim diem gan dung.

### Buoi 18: Sap xep co ban

- Muc tieu: hieu y tuong sap xep va dung `sort`.
- Thuc hanh: sap xep diem tang/giam, xep hang hoc sinh.

## Tuan 10: File text

### Buoi 19: Ghi file

- Muc tieu: luu ket qua ra file `.txt`.
- Noi dung: `ofstream`.
- Thuc hanh: luu danh sach hoc sinh.

### Buoi 20: Doc file

- Muc tieu: doc du lieu tu file de tiep tuc xu ly.
- Noi dung: `ifstream`.
- Thuc hanh: doc danh sach diem va tinh thong ke.

## Tuan 11: Du an cuoi khoa

### Buoi 21: Chon de tai va lap ke hoach

- Muc tieu: hoc sinh chon du an vua suc.
- Viec can lam: viet tinh nang chinh, chia viec thanh ham, tao menu.

### Buoi 22: Lap trinh ban dau

- Muc tieu: co phien ban chay duoc, du thieu tinh nang.
- Giao vien tap trung sua loi thiet ke: ham qua dai, bien dat ten kho hieu, menu roi.

## Tuan 12: Hoan thien va trinh bay

### Buoi 23: Hoan thien

- Muc tieu: sua loi, lam dep output, them kiem tra du lieu nhap.
- Yeu cau: chay thu it nhat 5 truong hop.

### Buoi 24: Demo

- Muc tieu: hoc sinh trinh bay san pham 3-5 phut.
- Noi dung demo: bai toan, tinh nang, phan code tam dac nhat, dieu se cai tien tiep.
